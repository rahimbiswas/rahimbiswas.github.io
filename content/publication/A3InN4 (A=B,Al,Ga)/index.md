abstract: >-
  The discovery of stable semiconductors with tunable electronic and
  optical properties is essential for developing optoelectronic
  technologies. Here, we performed first-principles calculations to
  investigate the cubic A3InN4 (A = B, Al, Ga), with particular emphasis
  on their stability, mechanical response, thermal characteristics,
  electronic structure, and light-matter interactions. All three
  compositions adopt the cubic P-43m structure, while their negative
  formation energies indicate energetic favorability. Phonon analysis
  demonstrates dynamical stability without imaginary modes across the
  Brillouin zone. The calculated elastic constants satisfy the cubic Born
  stability criteria, confirming mechanical stability, while the
  calculated elastic parameters reveal high stiffness and a ductile
  nature. A significant variation in thermal properties is observed, with
  the Debye temperature spanning 533.43–914.63 K and the estimated
  melting temperature reaching 3442±300 K. Electronic band structure
  calculations predict semiconducting behavior with GGA-PBE band gaps of
  3.402, 2.359, and 0.826 eV for B3InN4, Al3InN4, and Ga3InN4,
  respectively. Since GGA-PBE underestimates band gaps, additional HSE06
  hybrid-functional calculations were performed, which increase the gaps
  to 4.451, 3.415, and 1.866 eV, respectively, while preserving the
  B > Al > Ga trend. Their optical spectra exhibit strong absorption,
  conductivity, and pronounced dielectric responses in the ultraviolet
  region. The combined stability, tunable band gaps, and strong
  ultraviolet optical response suggest that A3InN4 (A = B, Al, Ga) are
  promising candidates for optoelectronic applications.
