abstract: >-
  Silicon-based perovskites have emerged as promising materials for applications in electronics, photovoltaics, optoelectronic systems, and high-temperature device technologies. In this work, a first-principles investigation is performed on silicon-based cubic ASiO3 (A = P, As, Sb, Bi) perovskites. The optimized lattice constants are calculated to be 3.57 Å for PSiO3, 3.61 Å for AsSiO3, 3.74 Å for SbSiO3, and 3.78 Å for BiSiO3, confirming their cubic structure within the GGA–PBE approximation. The Goldschmidt tolerance factors, together with the Born stability criteria, confirm the structural and mechanical stability of our studied compounds. The calculated bulk, shear, and Young's moduli indicate strong mechanical robustness, while Pugh's and Poisson's ratios reveal ductile behavior for PSiO3, AsSiO3, and BiSiO3 and brittle behavior for SbSiO3; ELATE-3D analysis further demonstrates anisotropic mechanical characteristics. Among the studied materials, the PSiO3 compound exhibits favorable machinability and low frictional behavior, indicating its strong potential for industrial machining applications. Electronic band structures and density of states indicate semiconducting properties, with band gaps of 1.589 eV for PSiO3, 1.885 eV for AsSiO3, 2.791 eV for SbSiO3, and 3.206 eV for BiSiO3 by using the HSE06 functional. Optical analyses show strong absorption and conductivity in the ultraviolet range and moderate absorption and conductivity in the visible range, with low reflectivity at higher energies. Among the studied compounds, BiSiO3 shows superior thermal barrier coating applications due to its distinctly low Debye temperature and high melting point. Also, the thermal properties displayed by SbSiO3, AsSiO3, and PSiO3 indicate that these compounds might still be serviceable where temperature demands are not extreme. Therefore, the lead-free ASiO3 (A = P, As, Sb, Bi) compounds emerge as potential materials for their semiconducting nature and optical responses for photovoltaic and optoelectronic devices and for applications involving high thermal applications.

summary: DFT study of lead-free cubic ASiO3 (A = P, As, Sb, Bi) perovskites. All four are mechanically stable semiconductors (HSE06 gaps 1.589–3.206 eV) with strong UV absorption; BiSiO3 shows strong thermal barrier coating potential.
