abstract: >-
  The development of structurally stable half-metallic perovskites
  with coupled magnetic and optoelectronic functionalities is highly
  desirable for next-generation opto-spintronic devices. In this study,
  first-principles calculations are performed to systematically
  investigate the structural, magnetic, mechanical, thermal, electronic,
  optical, and dynamical properties of cubic XEuO3 (X = K, Rb, Cs, Fr)
  perovskites. The investigated compounds exhibit an energetically
  favorable ferrimagnetic (FiM) ground state relative to competing
  magnetic configurations, while negative formation energies and
  positive cohesive energies support their thermodynamic stability
  and strong lattice cohesion. Spin-polarized electronic band
  structures reveal intrinsic half-metallic behavior with complete
  spin polarization at the Fermi level. Nearly integer total magnetic
  moments of approximately 4 μB and high estimated Curie temperatures
  demonstrate robust ferrimagnetic behavior. All compounds satisfy
  the Born stability criteria, with Young's moduli ranging from
  80.12 to 130.58 GPa and Vickers hardness values from 4.19 to
  13.14 GPa. The mechanical indicators classify KEuO3, RbEuO3,
  and CsEuO3 as ductile, whereas FrEuO3 exhibits brittle behavior.
  The calculated Debye temperatures range from 288.2 to 338.7 K,
  while the minimum thermal conductivity remains low at
  0.506-0.598 W m-1 K-1. Strong dielectric response, pronounced
  infrared and ultraviolet absorption, high optical conductivity,
  and substantial reflectivity highlight their broad optoelectronic
  activity. Moreover, the absence of imaginary phonon modes
  throughout the Brillouin zone confirms their dynamical stability.
  These combined characteristics suggest that XEuO3 (X = K, Rb,
  Cs, Fr) perovskites are promising theoretical candidates for
  future opto-spintronic applications.

summary: >-
  A comprehensive first-principles DFT investigation of the structural,
  magnetic, mechanical, electronic, optical, thermal, and dynamical
  properties of cubic XEuO3 (X = K, Rb, Cs, Fr) perovskites,
  revealing robust ferrimagnetism, intrinsic half-metallicity,
  complete spin polarization, and potential applications in
  next-generation opto-spintronic devices.
