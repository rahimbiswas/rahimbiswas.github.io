abstract: >-
  The development of structurally stable half-metallic perovskites
  with coupled magnetic and optoelectronic functionalities is highly
  desirable for next-generation opto-spintronic devices. In this study,
  first-principles calculations are performed to systematically
  investigate the structural, magnetic, mechanical, thermal, electronic,
  optical, and dynamical properties of cubic XEuO3 (X = K, Rb, Cs, Fr)
  perovskites. The investigated compounds exhibit an energetically
  favorable ferrimagnetic ground state, while negative formation
  energies and positive cohesive energies support their thermodynamic
  stability and strong lattice cohesion. Spin-polarized electronic
  band structures reveal intrinsic half-metallic behavior with
  complete spin polarization at the Fermi level. Nearly integer total
  magnetic moments of approximately 4 μB and high estimated Curie
  temperatures demonstrate robust ferrimagnetic behavior. All
  compounds satisfy the Born stability criteria, with Young's moduli
  ranging from 80.12 to 130.58 GPa and Vickers hardness values from
  4.19 to 13.14 GPa. KEuO3, RbEuO3, and CsEuO3 exhibit ductile
  mechanical behavior, whereas FrEuO3 exhibits brittle behavior.
  The calculated Debye temperatures range from 288.2 to 338.7 K,
  while the minimum thermal conductivity ranges from 0.506 to
  0.598 W m-1 K-1. The compounds also exhibit strong dielectric
  responses, pronounced infrared and ultraviolet absorption, high
  optical conductivity, and substantial reflectivity. Furthermore,
  the absence of imaginary phonon modes throughout the Brillouin
  zone confirms their dynamical stability. These findings suggest
  that cubic XEuO3 perovskites are promising theoretical candidates
  for potential opto-spintronic applications.
